# Casual dialogue

![Reference](reference.webp)

- Use for cozy bragging, gentle teasing, relaxed laziness, food, or affectionate physical comedy.
- Use one rounded cream-white bubble with a thin warm dark outline and two short lines.
- Use friendly dark-brown handwritten lettering at medium-large size.
- Place tiny red hearts or yellow wedges sparingly. Put motion-linked sound words beside the action.
- Store the bubble sentence in `core_text`; store sound words such as patting or rustling in `flavor_text`.
- Do not add heavy black poster outlines or aggressive speed lines.

Prompt fragment: Use a warm casual comic bubble with thin dark-brown outline, friendly rounded brown Chinese handwriting, loose two-line spacing, one tiny heart, and small motion-following flavor text near the action. Copy only the treatment, never the wording.
