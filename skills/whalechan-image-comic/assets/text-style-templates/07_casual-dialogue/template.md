# Casual dialogue

![Reference](reference.webp)

- Use for cozy bragging, gentle teasing, relaxed laziness, food, or affectionate physical comedy.
- Use one rounded cream-white bubble with a thin warm dark outline and two short lines.
- Use friendly dark-brown handwritten lettering at medium-large size.
- Place a tiny red heart only beside warm or affectionate lines, never beside panic, anger or deadpan lines; yellow wedges may mark surprise.
- Store the bubble sentence in `core_text`. Nonverbal marks are drawn symbols, not text; any written sound word goes in `core_text`.
- Do not add heavy black poster outlines or aggressive speed lines.

Prompt fragment: Use a warm casual comic bubble with thin dark-brown outline, friendly rounded brown Chinese handwriting, loose two-line spacing, a tiny heart only when the line is warm or affectionate, and small nonverbal motion marks near the action (no extra words). Copy only the treatment, never the wording.
