# Blue banner

![Reference](reference.webp)

- Use for confident improvised answers, blunt conclusions, slogans, or shameless declarations.
- Place a two-line headline across the upper third or upper half; keep the character below or partly beside it.
- Use very large rounded royal-blue letters, a thick white sticker outline, and a soft lavender-blue offset shadow.
- Keep tracking tight and line breaks punchy. Add at most two pairs of blue emphasis rays.
- Use one short `core_text` headline; avoid secondary dialogue unless essential.
- Do not center small body copy inside a conventional bubble.

Prompt fragment: Render one oversized two-line royal-blue banner headline with chunky rounded Chinese lettering, thick white sticker outline, pale lavender-blue offset shadow, tight spacing, and sparse blue emphasis rays. Copy only the lettering system, not the words or composition.
